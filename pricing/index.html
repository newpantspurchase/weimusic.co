Frequently asked questions

What can you help me with?

Music production, sound design, audio mixing, and music to picture (scoring and syncing music to video).

Do you teach online or in person?

Both - lessons run over Zoom or in person, whichever works better for you.

What software do you teach in?

Mainly Logic Pro, with Ableton Live as well - happy to work in whichever one you're more comfortable in, or help you get started if you haven't picked one yet.

What level should I be at to start?

Any level - from total beginners who've never opened a DAW before, to advanced producers looking to sharpen a specific skill.

Is there an age requirement?

No - lessons are open to all ages. Bringing production to as many different people as possible is part of the mission here.

What happens in the free first lesson?

It's a chance to meet, talk about what you want to get out of lessons, and make sure the pace and approach are the right fit - no cost, no obligation to continue.

Can I reschedule or cancel a lesson?

Life happens - just reach out by email as soon as you can and we'll find a new time that works.

Feel free to reach out if you have any specific questions.
